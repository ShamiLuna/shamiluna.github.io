Privacy Policy for Antishadow ASMR

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Antishadow ASMR respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifying information like your real name, physical address, or phone number. We may collect non-personal data to improve gameplay and deliver advertising:

  • Device Data: Device model, OS version, IP address, and game performance analytics (via Firebase Analytics).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) used by Google AdMob to serve ads.
  • Cloud Save Data: Anonymous user identifiers and game progression stats (stars, unlocked toys, levels completed) saved via Cloud Firestore.

2. Third-Party Services

Antishadow ASMR uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics & Cloud Firestore

3. Children's Privacy

Antishadow ASMR does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
